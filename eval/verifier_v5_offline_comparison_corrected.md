## 5. Trade-offs (read with the "unknown" row)

- **Final response vs. draft decision.** "False refusals" counts only questions whose final response is known. Four
  (P1) or five (P1+P6) answerable questions move to "needs a live redraft": the candidate rejected draft 1 for
  different reasons than v4, so the saved redraft (written from v4's feedback) does not stand for the candidate's
  redraft, or (q12 under P6) no redraft was ever made. Their final response is unknown; each could end as a
  refusal, a correct answer or a wrong answer. They are not counted as correct refusals or as answers.
- **P1 alone.** False acceptance: no change anywhere (labelled sets identical to v4; the factually wrong q12 draft
  is still accepted, as in v4). Benefit: citation parsing failures 21 → 0, supported true sentences accepted
  9 → 16 of 33, and one partially correct answer delivered (q06); no correct answer is gained among the known
  outcomes. Known false refusals 9 → 4, with 4 unknown.
- **P1 + P6.** Same as P1, plus the only accepted false sentence and the only delivered wrong answer (q12) are
  rejected: wrong answers delivered 1 → 0, unsupported or false sentences accepted 1 → 0. q12's final response is
  unknown (no saved redraft). P6 changed no labelled pair and no other real draft.
- The labelled sets contain no 【…】 citations and no table-backed absence claims, so they test that nothing else
  moves, not the benefit.

## 7. Unresolved issues

- The decisive outcomes (q02, q09, q10, q13 under P1; also q12 under P6) need live redrafts, i.e. real model calls,
  which were out of scope.
- Grades are assistant-generated and not yet confirmed by the owner.
- P6 is narrow by design: it only fires when a table caption, an exactly matched row variant and every requested
  cell line up; it would not catch absence claims backed by prose, or by tables whose caption words the claim
  does not repeat. Matching the plain "Moshi" row to "with Inner Monologue" relies on the "(no …)" sibling row.
- Root causes found in Phase 4 (named subject vs. "we" in the passage; whole-sentence + clause strictness) are
  untouched; they explain the false rejections of q04, q07 and q08, which no variant changes.

## 8. Recommendation

**Leave v4 frozen; no production change now.** Offline, both candidates are safe on the evidence available: P1
changes nothing in the labelled sets and only removes parsing failures; P6 removes the one false acceptance and
changes nothing else. But the outcome is inconclusive: with P1 the final response of four answerable questions
(five with P6) cannot be known without new model calls, and no correct answer is gained among the known
outcomes. P1 and P6 should be **accepted for further testing**, not deployed: a small, separately approved live
replay of q02, q09, q10, q12 and q13 under v5 (at most two calls each) would settle it.
